# 🌙 Caverns & Combat Mechanics

The subterranean mines form the core **action** pillar of Oakhaven — where you battle corrupted stardust creatures, cleanse mine shafts, quarry precious ores, and gather rare crafting materials.

## Dungeon Loop Mechanics

- **100 Floors / 4 Geological Strata**, each zone spanning ~25 floors with **procedurally generated rooms**.
- **Room Lockdowns:** defeat all hostiles in the chamber → doors unlock → proceed deeper.
- **Firefly Lantern = Illumination:** shafts are pitch black; your hip-lantern is required to spot lurking monsters and pathways — a signature tactical mechanic.
- **Strata Wardens:** Corrupted boss at the end of each stratum → defeat to unlock the regional waystone and mythic drops.
- **Grandmother's Shrines:** choose 1 temporary run buff per floor to bolster your survivability.

## Combat Systems

- **Unified Stamina Bar** (no separate HP gauge): taking hits drains Stamina directly; Stamina 0 = **collapse**.
- **Signature Weaponry:** Starlight Blade (3-hit rhythm combo), Mystic Scythe (wide AoE), Firefly Storm Lantern (ranged beam + light focus), and Colossal Whisk Hammer (armor-shattering slam). Evade incoming attacks with **Stardust Dash**.
- **Tactical Vulnerabilities:** each creature family possesses specific weaknesses (frost, flame, armor break, radiant light) — switch weapons tactically.

## Expedition Stakes

- Collapsing in cavern depths → forest spirits or Boris pull you back up to the clinic.
- **You forfeit a fraction** of unbanked foraged loot from that specific run, but **retain** all ores safely stored in surface chests as well as blueprint unlocks.
- No permanent game over — challenging dungeon combat without punitive cruelty.
