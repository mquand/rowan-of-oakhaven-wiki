# 🏮 Oakhaven Festivals & Events

The valley of Oakhaven breathes with life across four distinct seasons (`4 seasons × 28 days = 112 days`). Festivals are not mere diversions; they serve as **essential community milestones** where longstanding postwar grief is resolved, ancient shrines are purified, and Rowan's Hearth Eatery becomes the sanctuary uniting townsfolk.

---

## 🌸 1. Spring Cleansing & The Sacred Grove (Spring 15)

* **Time:** `09:00 – 22:00`, Spring 15.
* **Location:** Ancient Memorial Altar & Whispering Woods Edge.
* **Significance:** A solemn early-spring ceremony honoring postwar fallen comrades and cleansing toxic miasma that has choked frontier soil for a decade.
* **Key Activities:**
  1. **Communal Shrine Feast:** Cook 5 pure vegetable delicacies (lotus bulb herbal potage, bamboo rice, steamed forest mushrooms) as offerings at the Ancient Altar.
  2. **First-Harvest Lotus Tea:** Rowan brews fresh snow-lotus buds to serve elder townsfolk (Maeve, Captain Garrick).
  3. **Vanguard Candlelight Vigil:** Light floating lotus candles with Guildmaster Thorne and Valerie to remember the 18 fallen vanguard warriors.
* **Rewards & Unlocks:**
  - 🔓 **The Sacred Elder Herb Grove:** Permanently disperses toxic haze, unlocking access to legendary medicinal herb beds and wild star-honey apiaries.
  - ❤️ **+100 Friendship Points** with all town residents.
  - 🎖️ Unlocks the title: *"Keeper of Cleansing Flames"*.

---

## ☀️ 2. Adventurer Games & Firefly Night (Summer 18)

* **Time:** `10:00 – 23:00`, Summer 18.
* **Location:** Town Square & Firefly Lake Wharf.
* **Significance:** Honoring the courage of miners, scouts, and frontier guardians protecting the valley.
* **Key Activities:**
  1. **Sparring Exhibition:** Practice dodge rolls, parries, and combat drills against Guildmaster Thorne.
  2. **Speed Field-Cooking:** A 180-second challenge preparing combat ration boxes that grant ATK/DEF buffs.
  3. **Firefly Boat Ride (From 20:00):** Invite a close companion to row across the shimmering lake and release candlelit paper lanterns into the night.
* **Rewards & Unlocks:**
  - 🛡️ **Honorary Vanguard Badge:** Permanently lowers equipment repair and reforging fees by **15%** at Boris's Forge.
  - ❤️ **+200 Friendship Points** with your night-boat partner.
  - 📜 Exclusive combat recipe: *Hellfire Roast*.

---

## 🍂 3. Autumn Harvest Feast & Lantern Gala (Autumn 20)

> **The Grandest Festival of the Year:** Fusing bumper golden wheat harvests with traditional Mid-Autumn candlelit paper lanterns.

* **Time:** `12:00 – 24:00`, Autumn 20.
* **Location:** Town Square & The Hearth Eatery.
* **Key Activities:**
  1. **Autumn Master Chef Contest:** Present 3 dishes to judges Boris, Maeve, and Clara, evaluated on ingredient freshness and baking heat accuracy.
  2. **Lotus Lantern Parade:** Children and elders hang colored cellophane lanterns across cobblestone avenues.
  3. **Open Eatery Banquet:** Serve free pastries and lotus desserts to travelers and villagers alike.
* **Rewards & Unlocks:**
  - 🏆 **Golden Harvest Trophy:** Permanently raises selling prices of all dishes at the Hearth Eatery by **15%**.
  - 👘 Unlocks the festive costume: *"Harvest Moon Chef"*.
  - 📜 Unlocks traditional recipe: *Mid-Autumn Blossom Mooncake*.

---

## ❄️ 4. New Year Hearthfire Vigil (Winter 28 — New Year's Eve)

* **Time:** `18:00 – 02:00`, Winter 28.
* **Location:** The Hearth Eatery & Tavern.
* **Significance:** In the depths of winter cold, townsfolk gather inside Rowan's roaring hearth to share reflections and welcome the blossoming new year.
* **Key Activities:**
  1. **Grand Forest Hotpot Feast:** Rowan brews a massive bubbling cauldron of seasonal meats and root crops for the entire town.
  2. **New Year Reflections:** Townsfolk share intimate reflections on personal wounds healed throughout the year.
  3. **Pip's Lucky Seed Lottery:** Pip raffles mystery seed pouches carrying rare spring cultivars.
* **Rewards & Unlocks:**
  - 📖 Unlocks **Grandmother Amalia's Secret Tome Volume 2** (rare royal recipes).
  - 🌱 **+20% Growth Speed** for all greenhouse crops throughout the coming spring.
  - ❤️ **+150 Friendship Points** with all attending villagers.

## ⚓ 5. Weekly Recurring Events

### ⛵ Captain Garrick's Lakeshore Wharf (Sundays)
* **Time:** `07:00 – 17:00`, Sundays.
* **Wares:** Deep-sea fish, rare imported spices, and vintage Lo-fi vinyl records.

### 🔨 Town Rest & Maintenance Day (Wednesdays)
* **Time:** All Day Wednesday.
* **Perks:** Boris cools his forge for inspections; Clara opens free library study hours; Maeve offers 10% off crop seeds.
