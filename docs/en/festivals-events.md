# 🏮 Oakhaven Festivals & Progressive Regional Events

Oakhaven's festival calendar is not capped at 4 fixed days per year. Instead, it follows a **2-Tier Progressive Architecture**:
- **Tier 1 — 4 Local Oakhaven Festivals (Available from Year 1):** Hosted entirely within existing valley locations without requiring expansion structures.
- **Tier 2 — 4 Unlockable Regional Festivals (Year 2+):** Whenever Rowan completes a major map expansion project (Irrigation Canal, Ancient Forest Purification, Wolf-Tooth Cableway, Solaria Railway), Pip delivers an invitation the next morning, adding a new festival to the annual calendar (up to **8 festivals/year — exactly 2 per season, spaced 11–16 days apart**).

**Festival Rhythm & Calendar Priority Rules:**
- No rigid Activity Tickets. Daytime (10:00–19:00) is free for browsing stalls, playing minigames, or running the Pop-up Street Stall; Nighttime (20:00–23:00) hosts the shared community finale.
- Festivals avoid **Wednesdays (Boris Forge Shutdown)** and **Sundays (Garrick's Market)**; only **Winter 28 (New Year's Eve)** stays at year-end and **overrides Weekly Events**.
- The **Ancient Altar Purification Ritual** is triggered manually by the player from **22:00** on any non-festival night.

## 🌿 TIER 1: 4 LOCAL OAKHAVEN FESTIVALS (YEAR 1)

### 🌸 1. Spring Memorial & Canal Clearing Day (Spring 15 — Monday)
* **Location:** Whispering Woods Edge & Ancient Altar.
* **Atmosphere:** Quiet post-war remembrance combined with clearing winter boulders from Rowan's homestead irrigation ditches.
* **Main Activities:**
  1. **Help Boris & Colm Clear the Ditch:** In Year 1, smash 5 blocking boulders to **restore early-season irrigation to Rowan's farm** (Spring soil moisture buff + 50 Tokens; *does not replace the Region 2 Highland Aqueduct project*). From Year 2+, turns into a **Speed Stone-Breaking Contest** for ore and Tokens. *(Safety Net: Submit 30 Wood + 20 Stone at the Restoration Board if missed in Year 1).*
  2. **Ancient Altar Offering:** Present a 3-dish humble feast (Lotus Root Soup, Bamboo Rice, Wild Mushrooms) for +40 Tokens and rare spring seeds.

### 🎆 2. Lakeside Fireworks & Summer Night Market (Summer 19 — Friday)
* **Location:** Oakhaven Lake Pier & Observatory Hill.
* **Atmosphere:** A vibrant *Hanabi* celebration — Miner **Gomer** and Astronomer **Orion** craft fireworks from mine crystal powder.
* **Main Activities:**
  1. **Night Market & Custom Fireworks:** Enjoy grilled skewers, goldfish/firefly scooping, and fortune slips by day. Before 19:00, give Gomer **rare ores** (Gold, Mithril, Amethyst, Glowstone) to customize firework colors and patterns (flowers, shooting stars, fire dragons).
  2. **20:00 Lakeside Fireworks:** Pick a close NPC (or sit with friends) on the wooden pier to watch fireworks reflect across the water. Next morning, **Firework Shards / Glow-Quartz** wash up along the shore.

### 🎃 3. Harvest Masquerade & Pumpkin Lantern Night (Autumn 12 — Friday)
* **Location:** Oakhaven Town Square & The Hearth Courtyard.
* **Atmosphere:** A Dark Cozy *Harvest Masquerade* — regular streetlamps are dimmed, warm pumpkin lanterns line the paths, and villagers wear wooden/cloth masks while telling ghost stories around bonfires.
* **Main Activities:**
  1. **Trick-or-Treat for Village Kids:** In the evening, children (`Kael`, `Cora`, `Milo`, `Finn`, `Tilly`) visit The Hearth in costume; hand out homemade pumpkin pies, candies, or cookies for high friendship gains and Tokens/charms.
  2. **Shadow Night Market & Pumpkin Hunt:** **Morrigan** and masked merchants sell **Playful Masks**, **24h Transformation Potions** (hair color swap, cat ears, firefly trail steps next day), and **Mystery Bags**, alongside hunting 5–7 hidden pumpkin lanterns.

### 🍲 4. Solstice Bonfire & Communal Hotpot Night (Winter 28 — Sunday, New Year's Eve)
* **Location:** The Hearth Main Hall *(overrides Garrick's Sunday Market; Zephyr Sky Caravan opens 16:00–22:00 at Observatory Hill)*.
* **Atmosphere:** Heavy snow outside, warm hearthfire inside as the valley gathers to contribute ingredients to one giant communal pot.
* **Main Activities:**
  1. **The Communal Pot (3 Ingredient Slots):** Contribute Starch + Protein + Herb/Ferment. Year 1 unlocks **Grandma Amalia's Recipe Book Vol. 2** (if missed, Clara mails it next morning for a 300g retrieval fee); Year 2+ grants **Golden Spices & Bonus Tokens**. Grants +20 to +80 town-wide friendship plus **+50 bonus points for NPCs whose favorite dish matches pot ingredients**.
  2. **Midnight Fireside Toast (23:00):** Share warm apple cider and close out the year (+40 Tokens).

## 🚆 TIER 2: 4 UNLOCKABLE REGIONAL FESTIVALS

1. **Irrigation Canal Unlocked (Region 2 — Rice Terraces) → Paddy Planting & Skiff Race *(Spring 26 — Thursday)*:**
   - Celebrates fresh water reaching the terraces ahead of Summer rice planting; catch paddy fish, row skiffs for snow lotuses, and grill fresh fish along the banks.
2. **Herb Forest Unlocked (Region 3 — Eternal Forest) → Eclipse Night & Arcane Alchemy Market *(Summer 06 — Saturday)*:**
   - Herbalists, alchemists, and fortune-tellers set up glowing stalls: buy unusual mine buff potions, draw Tarot cards for seasonal luck, trade monster drops for hidden recipes, and release **Sky Lanterns** at night.
3. **Cableway Unlocked (Region 4 — Wolf-Tooth Peak) → Mist Market & Hot Springs *(Winter 12 — Friday)*:**
   - Trade rare ores and spiced ginger wine with highland miners and border scouts; soak in the **Herbal & Ginger Hot Springs** for a **Seasonal Blessing (`+15 Max Stamina & -25% Cold Damage` lasting the rest of Winter, separate from the 5 Combat Buff Slots)**.
4. **Railway Unlocked (Region 5 — Solaria Capital) → Royal Banquet & Capital Arena *(Autumn 25 — Thursday)*:**
   - Take the morning train with villagers (`Barnaby`, `Clara`, `Lumi`...) to **Solaria Gate Square**:
     - **Royal Master Chef Contest:** Cook 5-star dishes for **Lord Julian**; 3–5 stars award the Golden Trophy and a permanent **+15% dish selling price**.
     - **Guild Monster Trial Arena:** Hester & Valerius referee 3 waves of Mine Monsters / Clockwork Knights in a brightly lit arena (reusing existing monster sprites and AI); win Trophies, Weapons, or rare Soul Gems.
     - **Capital Specialty Stalls:** Purchase royal spices, rare seeds, and luxury tavern decor.

## 🍳 Pop-up Street Stall & Fair Tokens

- **Rowan Solo:** **100% Gold revenue** + **1 Fair Token per dish sold** (Year 1 max 60 dishes, 50g pitch fee).
- **Hire Youth (`Kael` or `Penny` — 50g fee):** **60% Gold revenue** and **0 Fair Tokens**, freeing Rowan to enjoy every festival activity.
- **Fair Tokens:** Single currency usable year-round at the **Town Hall Souvenir Locker**. Unused tokens convert to Gold at **26:00** on Winter 28 (mailed Spring 01). Missed items are sold by Captain Garrick from **Year 2 at 2.5x Gold price**.

## ⚓ Weekly Recurring Events

- **⛵ Captain Garrick's Sunday Lake Market (Sundays — Days 7, 14, 21, 28):** Deep-sea catch, foreign spices, Lo-fi vinyls, and past festival items from Year 2 *(overridden on Winter 28 New Year's Eve)*.
- **🔨 Forge Maintenance & Rest Day (Wednesdays — Days 3, 10, 17, 24):** Boris cools the forge; Clara's library opens free reading hours; Maeve discounts crop seeds by 10% (capped at 20 packets/type/week).
