# 🏮 Festivals & Seasonal Events

The Stardust Valley brims with heartwarming community life throughout the year — here are the recurring celebrations and festive gatherings you should never miss.

## Sunday Nautilus Market

Captain **Garrick's** spiral nautilus vessel docks at the harbor **once per week, on Sunday mornings**. At this market you can purchase:

- Rare vintage Lo-fi vinyl records for your shop phonograph
- Ancient artisanal sourdough yeast starters
- Rare exotic floral seeds

> Later in your journey, Garrick unlocks the secret passage to the Floating Archipelago.

## Autumn Pastry Jubilee & Lantern Festival

- **When:** Autumn 20 — the most grandly anticipated festival of the year.
- **Festivities:** Themed pastry competitions (Lotus Fragrance, Mooncakes, Starlight Pastries) + minigame lighting stained cellophane paper lanterns beneath the full moon.
- **Prizes:** Achieving the Gold Trophy unlocks the exclusive "Harvest Moon Chef" witch attire.

## Night of the Fireflies (Winter's Eve)

On the final night of Winter, villagers hang hundreds of glowing firefly lanterns around the Ancient Tree — illuminating the snowy night sky and bidding peaceful farewell to the passing year.

## Villager Birthdays & Celebration Cakes

Every valley citizen has a registered **birthday** on your shop calendar. Hand-decorate personalized **birthday celebration cakes** matching their favorite tastes to unlock special "Kindred Spirit" status and deeper dialogue memories.

## The Honor System Porch Stand & Mailbox

When you are out mining or foraging (shop sign flipped to `CLOSED`), regular visitors drop thoughtful thank-you notes and regional ingredients into your wooden mailbox — or purchase pastries directly at the **Porch Honor Stand**. You never have to worry about missing out on patron bonds.
