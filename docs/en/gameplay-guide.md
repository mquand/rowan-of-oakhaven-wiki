# 📖 Gameplay Guide — Life in Oakhaven

> **Game:** Rowan of Oakhaven  
> This guide consolidates core living mechanics: the daily clock cycle, four seasons, stamina limits, enchanted weather, and tool forge upgrades.

---

## The Daily Clock Cycle

A single in-game day spans **10 real-world minutes** (`1 game hour = 25 seconds`). Players arrange their daily schedule with complete freedom — there are no forced quests or stressful timers.

| Game Time | Phase | Real Time | Recommended Activities |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| 06:00 – 08:00 | Dawn | 50s | Check garden mailbox, collect seeds foraged by Pip |
| 08:00 – 12:00 | Morning | 100s | Farm chores (watering, harvesting, milling wheat) or mine entry |
| 12:00 – 17:00 | Afternoon | 125s | Visit Boris's forge, purchase seeds, delve deeper into shafts |
| 17:00 – 18:00 | Twilight | 25s | Drink tea for stamina buffs, prep bakery ingredients |
| 18:00 – 23:00 | Evening | 250s | **Shop peak hours** — flip sign to `[OPEN]`, listen to guests, brew teas |
| 23:00 – 02:00 | Late Night | 50s | Phonograph music, pin Polaroid memories, head to bed |
| After 02:00 | Exhaustion | — | Rowan falls asleep automatically; wakes up at 08:00 with 70% stamina |

**The Open/Closed Sign:** Flip the sign to `[OPEN]` to welcome patrons; flip to `[CLOSED]` whenever you prefer to mine, forage, or tend your garden — there is never any penalty for staying closed.

## The Four Seasons

A full calendar year comprises **112 days** (`4 seasons × 28 days`). Each season introduces unique crops, weather phenomena, and regular patrons.

- **Spring:** Tender sprouts emerge, snow-lotuses bloom, and gentle spring showers irrigate plots automatically.
- **Summer:** Harvest lumiberries, catch fireflies for lanterns; visitors favor iced fruit teas.
- **Autumn:** Silver wheat turns golden — **Autumn 20 hosts the Autumn Pastry Jubilee & Lantern Festival**.
- **Winter:** Outdoor soil freezes (crops thrive inside enchanted greenhouses), deep frost crystal mines open, and patrons crave piping-hot spiced brioche.

## Stamina Management

- **Maximum Base:** 100 points (expandable up to 160 with Starlight Crystal Brews).
- **Stamina Drain:** hoeing plot −2, watering −1/tile, breaking rocks −3, mining ores −5, crystal nodes −6, chopping timber −4.
- **Recovery Methods:** Warm Lotus Tea +35 · Solar Dawn Infusion +50 & mining speed buff · Honey Brioche +60 · sleeping before 00:00 restores 100%, late sleeping restores 80%.
- **Depletion:** Blacksmith Boris carries you safely back to your bed, waking at 08:00 with 70% stamina — no lost inventory items, no gold fines.

---

## Weather & Lighting Atmospheres

Four diurnal lighting transitions: **Dawn** (rosy amber mist) → **Midday** (warm sunlight) → **Twilight** (deep purple and amber hearth) → **Night** (magical indigo, paper lanterns light up automatically).

Four enchanted particle weather patterns: clear skies, **stardust rain** (irrigates the entire garden), **night mist** rolling over the lake, and **autumn leaf swirls**.

---

## Tool Upgrades at Boris's Forge

Bring cavern ores and gold to Boris to upgrade tools across 4 tiers (Wood/Rusty → Copper → Iron → Gold):

- **Watering Can:** 1 tile → 3-tile line → 3x3 square → 5x5 zone.
- **Mining Pickaxe:** common stone 3 hits → 2 hits + copper ore → 1 hit + iron/crystals → any node 1-2 hits.
- **Baking Oven:** 1 tray → 2 trays → 3 trays + heat preservation → 4 trays + ready bell chime.

## Movement & Tactile Interactivity

- 4-directional navigation; clean walking animation — accelerated via **tea speed buffs** and the Stardust Dash perk.
- Hold `Shift` to sprint (`180 px/s`), tap `Space` to dash once unlocked.
- The `carry` pose lifts harvested items above Rowan's head; cozy sitting chairs next to NPCs are fully supported.
- Crafting stations (tea brewing, pastry kneading) open **intimate tactile close-up interfaces** — allowing realistic manual gestures.
