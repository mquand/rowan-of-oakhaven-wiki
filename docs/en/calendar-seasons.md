# 📅 Calendar & The Four Seasons

A year in *Rowan of Oakhaven* consists of **4 seasons × 28 days = 112 days**. Each season shifts weather patterns, 92-species harvests, and hosts up to **2 Festivals per season** (**8 Festivals/year** across the 2-Tier Progressive Calendar).

## The Four Seasons & Festival Rhythm

| Season | Seasonal Characteristics | Key Festivals (2 per Season) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 🌸 **Spring** | Fresh shoots, snow-lotuses, spring showers auto-water tilled plots | • **Day 15:** Spring Memorial & Canal Clearing Day *(Tier 1)*<br/>• **Day 26:** Paddy Planting & Skiff Race *(Tier 2 — Irrigation Canal)* |
| ☀️ **Summer** | Wild berries, mountain herbs, and terraced rice cultivation | • **Day 06:** Eclipse Night & Arcane Market *(Tier 2 — Herb Forest)*<br/>• **Day 19:** Lakeside Fireworks & Night Market *(Tier 1)* |
| 🍂 **Autumn** | Golden silver-wheat, honey pumpkins, and orchard harvests | • **Day 12:** Harvest Masquerade & Pumpkin Night *(Tier 1)*<br/>• **Day 25:** Solaria Royal Banquet & Guild Arena *(Tier 2 — Railway)* |
| ❄️ **Winter** | Frozen soil (grow via Kael's Greenhouse), blizzards bringing Storm Strangers | • **Day 12:** Mist Market & Hot Springs *(Tier 2 — Cableway)*<br/>• **Day 28:** Solstice Bonfire & Communal Hotpot *(Tier 1 — NYE)* |

## Weather Phenomena

| Weather Type | In-Game Effect |
| :--- | :--- |
| ☀️ **Clear Sunlight** | Crisp light across the valley; ideal for Bento deliveries and gathering |
| 🌧️ **Rainy** | Natural rainfall — **automatically waters all outdoor tilled plots** |
| 🌫️ **Foggy Mist** | Dense morning/lake mist triggering outdoor Dynamic Encounters (Tier B) |
| 🍃 **Autumn Gale** | Swirling amber maple leaves marking peak orchard harvest |
| ❄️ **Blizzard / Heavy Rain** | Chance at 22:30 to shelter **Stormbound VIP Strangers** (*Valerius*, *Morrigan*) at the Inn |

## Diurnal Cycle

- **Dawn (06:00 – 08:00):** Rose-amber mist; automatic sprinklers fire at 06:00.
- **Daylight (08:00 – 17:00):** Prime hours for delving the 150-floor mine, forestry, and trade.
- **Twilight (17:00 – 19:00):** Warm twilight glow; prep evening hearth platters and inn check-in.
- **Nightfall (18:00 – 02:00):** Streetlamps ignite; optional **Evening Hearthside Service** runs 18:00 – 22:00. Staying past 02:00 AM triggers surface collapse.
