# 📅 Calendar & The Four Seasons

A complete year in Oakhaven consists of **4 seasons × 28 days = 112 days**. Each season introduces distinct environmental landscapes, meteorological shifts, and seasonal crops — naturally guiding your farming and mining goals across the year.

## The Four Seasons

| Season | Environmental Hallmarks | Recommended Pursuits |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 🌸 **Spring** | New buds emerge, snow-lotuses bloom, abundant showers (auto-irrigates plots) | Cultivate Lotus Tea, Starlight Lavender |
| ☀️ **Summer** | Ripe Lumiberry harvest, catching twilight fireflies for lanterns | Brew frosty fruit teas for patrons |
| 🍂 **Autumn** | Silver wheat turns golden amber across the valley | **Day 20: Autumn Pastry Jubilee & Lantern Festival**; grow Honey Pumpkins |
| ❄️ **Winter** | Outdoor topsoil freezes (only greenhouse cultivation continues) | Deep frost crystal mine shafts unlock; visitors crave spiced teas and hot brioche |

## Enchanted Weather Phenomena

| Weather Type | In-Game Effect |
| :--- | :--- |
| ☀️ Clear Sunlight | Crisp diagonal god-rays filtering through oak canopies |
| 🌧️ Stardust Rain | Shimmering luminescent rain — **automatically waters every tilled garden plot** |
| 🌫️ Evening Lake Mist | Soft rolling vapor drifting across lake waters |
| 🍃 Autumn Gale | Swirling amber maple leaves dancing through town streets |

## Diurnal Lighting Progression

- **Dawn (06:00–08:00):** Soft rose-orange hue, early morning dew.
- **Daylight (08:00–17:00):** Gentle golden sunlight.
- **Twilight (17:00–19:00):** Deep violet and warm honey-amber glow.
- **Nightfall (19:00–02:00):** Arcane indigo night skies — town lanterns and hearth candles flicker to life automatically.
