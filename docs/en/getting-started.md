# 🌱 Getting Started

> New to Oakhaven? This guide walks you through your first few days — there is no "wrong way", only gentle steps to settle into valley life.

## Who Are You?

You play as **Rowan**, a young apprentice witch who recently left the Royal Arcane Academy, returning to the Stardust Valley to inherit the rustic tea and bakery shop left by her late **Grandmother Amalia**. Accompanying you are your trusty companions: **Pip** the pygmy owl and **Mallow** the calico cat, alongside Grandmother's cherished "Tattered Secret Grimoire".

## Your Very First Day

1. **Flip the `[OPEN]` sign** outside your door to welcome visitors — you can open the shop at any hour you wish.
2. **Head to the backyard garden:** till the soil, plant starter seeds (Silver Wheat, Lotus Tea Buds), and water your plots.
3. **Milled wheat at the Windmill** into Fresh Baking Flour — essential dough for the oven.
4. **Twilight evening:** open the tea shop, listen to travelers' stories, and brew tea & bake pastries guided by emotional clues.
5. **Rest and sleep** to restore full Stamina and welcome the dawn of a new day.

## Daily Routine Cycle

| Time of Day | Recommended Activities |
| :--- | :--- |
| Morning | Check mailbox for Pip's seeds, water the garden plots |
| Midday | Choose your focus: tending crops, spelunking mines, or early shop hours |
| Evening | Open the tea salon for visitors, or explore deep shafts (flip sign to `CLOSED`) |
| Night | Sleep to restore Stamina, let crops grow, and advance seasonal calendar |

## Early Game Survival Tips

- **Stamina** is your only limiting resource. Running out simply results in passing out, where blacksmith Boris helps you back to bed — no death penalties, no lost items.
- **Save your progress** at any time.
- **You cannot lose:** serving the wrong brew elicits gentle hints rather than anger; neglected crops may wilt, but can easily be rejuvenated.
- **Drink your own tea** to restore Stamina and gain helpful buffs (swift walking, night vision in deep mines).
- **Unlock new realms:** gather materials to restore regional waystones (stardust carriage, suspension bridges, magma lifts) connecting the Five Realms.
