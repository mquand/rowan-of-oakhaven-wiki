# 🌱 Getting Started

> New to Oakhaven? This guide accompanies you through your first days — getting familiar with four-season farming, hazardous deep-mine combat, and firing up the Hearth Eatery & Tavern.

## Who Are You?

You play as **Rowan**, a resilient youth who fled the soul-draining industrial grind (burnout) of the imperial capital Solaria to return to peaceful Oakhaven Valley. You inherit the weathered homestead and rustic **Hearth Eatery & Tavern** left behind by your late **Grandmother Amalia**, alongside her grease-stained secret cookbook.

By your side are **Pip** the pygmy owl (bringing dawn letters and seeds) and **Mallow** the calico cat (curling up by the hearth fire). Your mission is to cultivate four-season crops, delve into the dangerous 150-floor mine for precious ores, fire up the kitchen stoves each evening to serve heartwarming meals to neighbors, and lead the post-war reconstruction of Oakhaven.

## Your Very First Day

1. **Crisp Morning (06:00):** Check the front porch mailbox for Pip's seeds, till the garden behind the tavern, plant spring crops, and water your soil.
2. **Afternoon Exploration (12:00):** Set willow fish traps by the stream, visit **Boris Barnaby** at the forge for basic tools and weapons, and meet the villagers around the square.
3. **Milling & Ingredient Prep:** Grind harvested wheat at the Windmill into fresh flour, prep seasonal vegetables for the evening dinner service.
4. **Lighting the Evening Stoves (18:00):** Flip the `[OPEN]` sign to welcome weary miners and villagers. Bake fresh breads, simmer hearty stews, and serve comfort dishes that dissolve defenses and heal real-world wounds.
5. **Resting at Rowan's Private Loft (Before 02:00):** Retreat to your upstairs loft to sleep before midnight exhaustion hits, restoring full Stamina and Health (HP) for the coming dawn.

| Time of Day | Recommended Activities |

| **Morning (06:00 - 12:00)** | Receive Pip's mail, tend crops (92 seasonal, special-region & ornamental species), harvest produce, care for livestock. |

## Survival Tips & Core Mechanics

- **Stamina (270, Cap 450) & Health (HP 100, Cap 250):**
  - Tilling, chopping, and mining consume Stamina. Reaching 0 causes exhaustion (50% movement speed penalty). Increase your Stamina cap (+30/milestone) via 3 Aurelia tonics and 3 Ancient Altar milestones.
  - Monster attacks deplete HP. Increase your HP cap (+30/milestone) by defeating Mine Bosses for the first time at floors `30, 60, 90, 120, 150`.
  - Reaching 0 in HP or Stamina (or passing `02:00 AM`) causes you to pass out and wake up the next morning with **50% HP & 50% Stamina**.
- **Storage Chests & Craft from Chests:**
  - Place Wooden or Iron Chests (`36 slots/chest`, `x99/x999` stacking) in the yard, mine entrance, or kitchen. Cooking and crafting automatically pull ingredients from linked chests.
- **Rescue Service & Lost & Found:**
  - **Passing out above ground / past `02:00 AM`:** **Colm** carries you back to bed, charging 10% Gold (max `1,000g`) for Aurelia's clinic with **zero item loss**.
  - **Collapsing in the 150-floor Mine:** **Guildmaster Thorne's** rescue squad pulls you to Aurelia's clinic, charging 10% Gold (max `1,000g`). Raw ores and monster loot from that run are **permanently lost**, while **weapons, armor, and tools** are sent to **Valerie's Lost & Found** counter to reclaim with Gold or **Rescue Badges** (from Expedition Lunchbox orders).
- **Eat to Gain Combat Buffs:** Cook and consume your own meals for immediate HP/Stamina recovery and combat buffs (Attack, Defense, Move Speed, Mining Luck).
- **Purification Feasts at Ancient Altars:** Offer traditional meals at battleground memorial altars across the valley to lay lingering spirits to rest and liberate blighted lands.
