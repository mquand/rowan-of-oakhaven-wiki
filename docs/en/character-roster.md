# 👥 Character Roster, Real-world Lore & Heart Events

> **Game:** Rowan of Oakhaven  
> **Narrative Philosophy:** Every inhabitant of Oakhaven is far more than a flat stereotype. Each carries a **real-world psychological wound**: the exhausting burden of caring for a dementia-stricken elder, unexpressed brotherly love trapped behind masculine stubbornness, the lingering survivor's guilt of wartime veterans, or an orphan's terror of abandonment. The hearty meals Rowan cooks at the Hearth Eatery serve as the emotional bridge that unlocks their vulnerabilities and initiates profound healing.

---

## 1. Protagonist & Cherished Companions

### 🌸 Rowan (Hearthkeeper & Frontier Delver)
* **Role:** Player-controlled protagonist.
* **Background:** A weary survivor of metropolitan burnout from the industrial capital of Solaria, where human beings were reduced to disposable cogs. Inheriting her late grandmother Amalia's weathered cottage, Rowan begins with empty hands: tilling stubborn soil, battling lethal horrors across 150 treacherous mine floors, and lighting the kitchen fire each night to nourish her frontier community.
* **Inner Conflict:** Imposter Syndrome — haunted by the dread that she is merely running away from failure and unworthy of her grandmother's towering culinary legacy.

### 🦉 Pip (Pygmy Owl Messenger)
* **Role:** Static morning mail courier & rare seed forager.
* **Behavior:** Perches loyally on the mailbox post at dawn. Does not follow Rowan into the wilderness. Delivers letters, town bulletin notices, and mysterious wild seeds gathered from the Whisperwood glades.

### 🐱 Mallow (Calico Hearth Familiar)
* **Role:** Eatery guardian & cozy companion.
* **Gameplay Mechanic (Lucky Hearth Buff):** When curled up asleep beside the tavern fireplace, activates the passive *"Lucky Hearth"* buff: reduces dish burning probability by 15% and increases dining patron gold tips by 10%.
* **Behavior:** Sought shelter beside the tavern hearth during a stormy gale and claimed it as home. Naps peacefully on the wooden counter and rubs affectionately against dining patrons.

### 👵 Grandmother Amalia (Guiding Legacy & Spirit)
* **Role:** Late culinary artisan whose handwritten grimoire guides Rowan.
* **Core Philosophy:** *"Cooking is not about showing off technical virtuosity; it is about making the soul who eats your meal feel that they are worthy of living and being loved."*

## 2. Oakhaven Citizens & Narrative Depth

---

### ⚒️ Boris Barnaby (Master Blacksmith) & ⛏️ Bram Barnaby (Deep Mine Forager)
> **Real-world Theme:** Dysfunctional brotherly bond — wounded male pride, inability to communicate love, and lingering trauma.

* **Boris Barnaby (Elder Brother - Age 52):**
  - *Psychological Wound:* A mine collapse on Floor 40 ten years ago crushed Bram's leg. Boris blamed himself for calling the blast, abandoned cave expeditions, and turned into an overbearing, controlling guardian who treats Bram like a fragile cripple.
  - *Signature Comfort Dish:* **Dwarf Ironforge Goulash (Slow-braised Beef & Beet Stew)** — the hearty recipe their late mother cooked for them in their youth.
* **Bram Barnaby (Younger Brother - Age 45):**
  - *Psychological Wound:* Resents being pitied. Bram dives recklessly into deeper mine shafts not out of greed, but to scream in Boris's face that he is still an independent, capable man who refuses pity.
  - *Signature Comfort Dish:* **Miner's Sourdough Loaf & Earth Root Stew**.
* **Heart Events Arc:**
  - *2 Hearts:* Heated argument at the anvil; Boris confiscates Bram's custom pickaxe, prompting Bram to storm off to the riverside in bitter silence.
  - *4 Hearts:* Rowan discovers Bram massaging his throbbing leg in the cold shadows of Floor 20, confessing that hearing Boris frantically hammer away rocks for 3 days was the only thing that kept him breathing under the rubble; unlocks **Deep-Vein Mineral Scouting**.
  - *6 Hearts:* Penny tasks Rowan with uncovering an old tin box containing their mother's original goulash recipe and a letter pleading for them to support one another.
  - *8 Hearts (Reconciliation Feast):* Rowan hosts a family dinner serving piping-hot Goulash. The hardened dwarven brothers weep openly, shattering a decade of estrangement and unlocking the **Advanced Refining Forge**.

---

### 🌾 Grandma Maeve (Elder Herbalist) & 🕯️ Little Kael (Matchstick Boy)
> **Real-world Theme:** Found family beyond bloodlines; the fragility of elder years and an orphan's terror of abandonment.

* **Grandma Maeve (Age 74):**
  - *Psychological Wound:* Lost her entire family to miasma plague 30 years ago. Crippling osteoarthritis torments her every winter, but she secretly refuses expensive medicines, saving every copper coin for Kael's future education and apprenticeship.
  - *Signature Comfort Dish:* **Sweet Pumpkin Lotus Potage & Sun Daisy Tea**.
* **Little Kael (Age 10):**
  - *Psychological Wound:* Shivered on frozen riverbanks peddling matches before Maeve took him in. Haunted by acute abandonment trauma: terrified that if Maeve passes away, he will be cast back into the freezing dark. Overworks himself relentlessly to prove he is worth keeping.
  - *Signature Comfort Dish:* **Seven-Color Faerie Birthday Cake** (Kael never knew his biological birthday; Maeve celebrates the day she adopted him).
* **Heart Events Arc:**
  - *3 Hearts:* Kael breaks down crying after dropping seed packets into muddy gutters; Rowan helps him wash the seeds and serves warm comforting broth.
  - *4 Hearts (Basic Greenhouse):* Grandma Maeve entrusts Rowan with the key to the **Four-Seasons Nursery (Basic Greenhouse)** for out-of-season farming.
  - *6 Hearts (Winter Blizzard):* Maeve suffers sudden chest pain during a gale. Kael braves the storm to pound on Rowan's tavern door, leading to a desperate midnight medical rescue.
  - *8 Hearts (The Hidden Passbook):* Kael uncovers Maeve's secret savings labeled *"For Kael's Carpentry Apprenticeship"*. The two embrace in tears. Unlocks the **Automated Plant Propagation Station**.

### 🛡️ Adventurer's Guild Leadership: Thorne & Valerie
> **Real-world Theme:** Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD), survivor's guilt, and finding a renewed sense of purpose.

* **Guildmaster Thorne (Age 50):**
  - *Psychological Wound:* Veteran vanguard captain bearing an iron prosthetic leg. Fifteen years ago, a catastrophic command error in the Sunken Necropolis wiped out his entire 18-soldier scouting squad. He founded the Oakhaven Rescue Outpost with a binding oath: *"No one is ever left behind in the dark."*
  - *Signature Comfort Dish:* **Campfire Spiced Jerky Stew**.
* **Dispatcher Valerie (Age 32 - Lost & Found Counter):**
  - *Psychological Wound:* Retired sharpshooter who suffered permanent unilateral hearing loss and severe acoustic shock (PTSD) from high-explosive ordnance. Meticulously catalogs lost adventurer belongings behind a quiet desk, secretly hoping to recover relics of fallen comrades.
  - *Signature Comfort Dish:* **Snow Fungus & Pork Rib Broth**.
* **Heart Events Arc:**
  - *4 Hearts:* Thorne stands silently in the rain before the Ancient Memorial Altar at the forest edge on the anniversary of his lost battalion; unlocks the **Valley Monster Bounty Board**.
  - *8 Hearts (Healing the Survivors):* Rowan cooks a nutritious frontline feast. Thorne and Valerie finally release the ghosts of their past, reducing rescue penalties by 50% and unlocking the **Deep-Mine Rescue Strike Force** (recovering 100% dropped gear via ration drops).

### 🔭 Professor Orion & 📚 Clara (Astronomy Tower)
> **Real-world Theme:** Cognitive decline / early-stage Alzheimer's, caregiver burnout, and filial sacrifice.

* **Professor Orion (Age 68):**
  - *Psychological Wound:* Slowly losing his memory to degenerative dementia. While forgetting village names and basic daily routines, he stays awake night after night beside his brass telescope, desperately searching for the fleeting comet he named after his late wife, Elise, before his mind empties completely.
  - *Signature Comfort Dish:* **Astral Berry Glazed Tart**.
  - *Gameplay Mechanic:* Supplies **3-Day Meteorological Advance Forecasts** at 4 Hearts (warning against blizzards and miasma rain); triggers the **Meteor Shower Event** to collect fallen surface crystals at 8 Hearts.
* **Clara (Age 24 - Head Librarian):**
  - *Psychological Wound:* Tore up an elite university scholarship in Solaria to stay behind and care for her fading father. Constantly oscillates between profound filial love and suffocating grief over her forfeited youth.
  - *Signature Comfort Dish:* **Earl Grey Jasmine Tea & Crispy Butter Biscuits**.
* **Heart Events Arc:**
  - *4 Hearts:* Orion wanders into the moonlit fog, mistaking distant lanterns for his deceased wife; Clara tracks him down in tears; unlocks **Ancient Manuscript Decryption Service**.
  - *8 Hearts (Constellation of Remembrance):* Rowan and Clara reconstruct Elise's star charts; Orion experiences a moment of lucid clarity, holding Clara's hand in heartfelt gratitude. Unlocks the **Ancient Mythic Recipe Archives**.

### ⚓ Captain Garrick & 🎣 Young Finn
* **Garrick (Age 70):** Color-blind, weather-beaten sky-sailor who feels like an obsolete piece of driftwood stranded ashore.
  - *Gameplay Mechanic:* Opens **Mist Lake Cargo Boat Dock** at 4 Hearts; clears the maritime trade route to import exotic spices at 8 Hearts.
* **Finn (Age 12):** Determined grandson fishing day and night to earn back the heirloom Silver Compass Garrick pawned to pay for his late father's medicine.
  - *Gameplay Mechanic:* Unlocks **Fishing Rod & Lake Minigame** at 4 Hearts; supplies **Bioluminescent Fish** for night-vision recipes in deep mines at 8 Hearts.
* **Signature Comfort Dish:** **Smoked Forest Salmon & Crisp Frostmint Tea**.

### 🌿 Lumi (Whisperwood Glade Kemomimi-Elf)
* **Psychological Wound:** Unique hybrid bearing both snow-fox ears and elven ears. Ostracized by both woodland fae and humans as an anomaly. Craves human warmth yet terrified of rejection.
* **Gameplay Mechanic (Whispering Forest Guide):**
  - *4 Hearts:* Keen fox ears forecast exact locations of rare herbs and wild fungi based on weather and moon phases.
  - *8 Hearts:* Guides Rowan deep into the **Deep Forest Shrine** to harvest ancient luminescent mushrooms and sacred flora.
* **Signature Comfort Dish:** **Calming Lotus Infusion & Steamed Coconut Rice Cakes**.

### 🧺 Penny (Itinerant Pastry Courier)
* **Psychological Wound:** Orphaned niece raised awkwardly by bachelor uncles Boris and Bram. Yearns to build an independent motorized bakery cart.
* **Gameplay Mechanic (Takeaway & Delivery):**
  - *4 Hearts:* Opens **Plaza Rolling Pastry Cart** in Oakhaven Market Square.
  - *8 Hearts:* Operates the **Town Hot Meal Delivery Service**, couriering hot boxed lunches and beverages to miners and busy villagers across the valley.
* **Signature Comfort Dish:** **Wild Berry Shortcake & Chilled Strawberry Infusion**.

### 🩺 Aurelia (Town Medic) & ⚔️ Marlo (Nightwatch Sentinel)
* **Aurelia:** Disillusioned physician who fled royal medical corruption to treat the impoverished, struggling with scarce supplies.
  - *Gameplay Mechanic:* Brews **Vitality Healing Broth** to relieve chronic pains at 2 Hearts; supplies deep-wound salves and stamina tonics at 6 Hearts.
* **Marlo:** Self-effacing nightwatchman who sends 90% of his pay to support younger siblings back home, finding his only solace at Rowan's midnight tavern table.
  - *Gameplay Mechanic (Night Security):* Patrols the valley at night; protects the Eatery and the front-porch "Honor Stand" from nocturnal theft (50% theft reduction at 4 Hearts, 100% immunity at 8 Hearts).

## 3. Newly Expanded Oakhaven Inhabitants

### 🏛️ Elder Oakwood (Town Mayor)
* **Psychological Wound:** "Tired Hero" syndrome. Governed and sheltered Oakhaven single-handedly through the Great Blight thirty years ago; now elderly and exhausted, yet terrified that stepping down will cause the frontier town to collapse.
* **Signature Comfort Dish:** **Whisper Hearth Herbal Tea & Brioche Bread**.
* **8 Hearts Milestone:** Relinquishes absolute executive duty to establish the democratic Town Council; unlocks the **Town Restoration Upgrade Board**.

### 🪵 Cedar (Master Bear-kin Carpenter)
* **Psychological Wound:** Banished from his mountain bear-kin clan for refusing battle axes; branded a coward yet steadfast in his conviction that raising a sturdy roof against winter blizzards takes more courage than slaughtering neighbors.
* **Signature Comfort Dish:** **Sweet Pumpkin & Lotus Potage with Wild Honey**.
* **8 Hearts Milestone:** Rebuilds the Ancient Timber Viaduct into Whisperwood; unlocks **Home & Hearth Eatery Structural Expansions**.

### 🎶 Wren (Wandering Halfling Bard)
* **Psychological Wound:** Lost his singing voice for three years following the sack of his homeland; suffers crippling social anxiety and stage fright whenever he is outside his musical trance.
* **Signature Comfort Dish:** **Wild Berry Shortcake Scone & Chilled Frostberry Brew**.
* **8 Hearts Milestone:** Debuts 'Lullaby of Oakhaven' at the Harvest Gala; unlocks the **Tavern Ambience Jukebox**.

### 🌾 Hazel (Livestock Matron) & 🐑 Tilly (Little Shepherdess)
* **Hazel:** Abandoned by her merchant spouse who vanished into metropolitan Solaria; fiercely distrustful of communal aid out of betrayal trauma.
  - *Comfort Dish:* **Warm Honeyed Cow's Milk & Crispy Wheat Loaf**.
  - *Milestone:* Founds the Valley Ranching Cooperative; unlocks purchasing cows, chickens, and sheep for dairy, egg, and wool production.
* **Tilly (Age 10 - Sheep-kin):** Gravely traumatized by a winter wolf attack that sheared one of her horns; battles acute nyctophobia while trying to prove her courage to Kael and Finn.
  - *Comfort Dish:* **Warm Honeyed Milk & Four-Season Rainbow Cake**.
  - *Milestone:* Conquers her dread of the dark to rescue an orphaned lamb; unlocks the **Rainbow Wool Dyeing Workshop**.

### 🏺 Bramble (Goblin Provisioner at 'Treasure Urn')
* **Psychological Wound:** Ostracized by Solaria's Merchant Conclave for refusing to sell tainted tonics to the poor; labeled an outcast by goblins and humans alike for holding to honesty.
* **Signature Comfort Dish:** **Honeyed Rolled Oat Porridge**.
* **8 Hearts Milestone:** Launches the Oakhaven Saturday Night Bazaar; unlocks exotic foreign seeds and specialty tools.

### 🌿 Rosemary (Ancient Oakhaven Shrine Keeper / Memorial Altars)
* **Psychological Wound:** Dryad — living avatar of Oakhaven's ancient Mother Oak. Watches helplessly as Blighted War Miasma rots deep subterranean rootways, dreading the day her consciousness dissolves as the oak withers.
* **Signature Comfort Dish:** **White-Leaf Lotus Tea & Candied Lotus Seeds**.
* **8 Hearts Milestone:** Performs the Great Memorial Feast with Rowan to purge ancient miasma; unlocks **Ancient Memorial Altars** and purifies blighted wastelands.

### 🛡️ Valerius (Exiled Knight) & 🔮 Morrigan (Recluse Hedge Witch)
* **Valerius:** Former royal guard commander framed for treason after exposing imperial corruption; winter storm guest who settles permanently as Captain of Oakhaven's Guardians.
  - *Comfort Dish:* **Oak-Charred Iron Steak & Red Wine Beef Stew**.
  - *Gameplay Mechanic:* Guards town gates by day; opens **Combat Dojo** teaching Rowan Shield Parry at 4 Hearts and Master Parry Counter at 8 Hearts.
* **Morrigan:** Solitary swamp witch ostracized worldwide due to superstitious dread; prickly and intimidating outside, but secretly a gentle nurse to wounded woodland wildlife.
  - *Comfort Dish:* **Frostmint Breeze Tea & Wild Glade Berries**.
  - *Gameplay Mechanic:* Concocts weapon-coating monster toxins at 4 Hearts; unlocks **Critical Strike Elixir** and permanent Miasma Antidote at 8 Hearts.

## 4. Greater Realms & Cosmic Thresholds

### ⚒️ Khaz-Drakor (The Dwarven Megalopolis)
* **High Smith Grimhild:** Foster mother of Boris and Bram; legendary dwarven matriarch who spent fifteen years hammering away maternal guilt after driving her sons away with rigid strictness.
  - *Comfort Dish:* **Dwarf Ironforge Goulash**. Unlocks Masterwork **Mithril Smithing** at 8 Hearts.
* **Cogsworth (Steam Engineer):** Slender young dwarf ostracized by conservative smiths for pursuing pneumatic valves and clockwork; only one fluent in Chrono's rhythm.
  - *Comfort Dish:* **Garlic Butter Roasted Spuds**. Crafts **Automated Field Sprinklers** at 4 Hearts; completes the **Minecart Express** ore transport rail system at 8 Hearts.
* **Ember (Lava Basin Chef):** Energetic fire salamander chef barred from metropolitan culinary guilds due to racial prejudice; Rowan's friendly cooking rival.
  - *Comfort Dish:* **Netherfire Charbroiled Steak**. Exchanges fire spices at 4 Hearts; transfers the superheated **Extreme Heat Magma Grate** at 8 Hearts.
* **Chrono (Ancient Clockwork Automaton):** Operates the town bell to signal meal hours and displays buff countdown timers on HUD at 4 Hearts; bell resonance boosts tavern revenue by 20% at 8 Hearts.

### 🌲 Sylva-Fae (The Ancient Canopy)
* **Thistle (Nocturnal Moth Ranger):** Silver-violet winged moth-folk whose sensitive eyes sting under bright lanterns; lives isolated in twilight canopy hours.
  - *Comfort Dish:* **Thousand-Star Lavender Infusion**. Gathers **Luminescent Silk** at 4 Hearts and **Golden Silk** at 8 Hearts for Elora.
* **Fern (Ancient Root Healer - Age 800):** Blind mentor of Sylvan who perceives all forest life through subterranean root mycorrhizae after losing his eyes to Blighted War Miasma.
  - *Comfort Dish:* **Lotus Blossom Root Potage**. Teaches **Primeval Herbal Codex** recipes at 6 Hearts; guides permanent soil miasma purification rites at 8 Hearts.
* **Elora & Sylvan:** Tremor-afflicted royal dressmaker and devoted botanist seeking ancient remedies to preserve her artisan hands.
  - *Elora:* Weaves **Expanded Adventurer Pouch (+12 slots)** at 4 Hearts; tailors the **Eternal Winter Gala Robe** at 8 Hearts.
  - *Sylvan:* Opens **Herbal Essential Oil Extractor** at 4 Hearts; harvests rare **Millennial Roots** at 8 Hearts.

### 🏯 Solaria (The Royal Capital of Light)
* **Iris (Royal Chronicler):** Auburn-haired scholar balancing precarious towers of tomes; torn between royal imperial censorship and her pledge to record authentic history.
  - *Comfort Dish:* **Earl Grey Jasmine Tea & Spiced Ginger Biscuits**. Opens **Deep Mine Relic Appraisal** at 4 Hearts; completes the **Valley Historical Museum** at 8 Hearts.
* **Valen (Vegetarian Vampire Noble):** VIP 2nd-floor inn resident; orders double-priced midnight dessert banquets and herbal wine at 4 Hearts; signs the Ancient Castle Midnight Feast Contract at 8 Hearts.
* **Kassandra (Wind Witch in Exile):** Opens **Sky Express 2-Hour Inter-Realm Air Delivery** at 4 Hearts; gifts Rowan the **Enchanted Flying Broom** at 8 Hearts.

### ✨ Aethelgard (The Floating Elysium)
* **Solace (Celestial Gate Guardian):** Radiant astral spirit watching the celestial threshold for millennia; surrendered mortal memories until Rowan's lotus tea re-awakens ancient recollections of having once been a humble baker.
  - *Comfort Dish:* **Calming Lotus Infusion**. Permanently unseals the **Gates of Aethelgard** linking all 5 realms at 8 Hearts.
* **Lapis (Subterranean Mermaid):** Trades cloud lake crystal sea salts at 4 Hearts; supplies glowing pearls to craft poison-resistant jewelry at 8 Hearts.
* **Lapis:** Deep-sea mermaid journeying onto dry land, captivated by warm pastries that dissolve on the tongue.
