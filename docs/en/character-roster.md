# 👥 Character Roster, Real-world Lore & Heart Events

> **Game:** Rowan of Oakhaven  
> **Narrative Philosophy:** Every inhabitant of Oakhaven is far more than a flat stereotype. Each carries a **real-world psychological wound**: the exhausting burden of caring for a dementia-stricken elder, unexpressed brotherly love trapped behind masculine stubbornness, the lingering survivor's guilt of wartime veterans, or an orphan's terror of abandonment. The hearty meals Rowan cooks at the Hearth Eatery serve as the emotional bridge that unlocks their vulnerabilities and initiates profound healing.

---

## 1. Protagonist & Cherished Companions

### 🌸 Rowan (Hearthkeeper & Frontier Delver)
* **Role:** Player-controlled protagonist.
* **Background:** A weary survivor of metropolitan burnout from the industrial capital of Solaria, where human beings were reduced to disposable cogs. Inheriting her late grandmother Amalia's weathered cottage, Rowan begins with empty hands: tilling stubborn soil, battling lethal horrors across 150 treacherous mine floors, and lighting the kitchen fire each night to nourish her frontier community.
* **Inner Conflict:** Imposter Syndrome — haunted by the dread that she is merely running away from failure and unworthy of her grandmother's towering culinary legacy.

### 🦉 Pip (Pygmy Owl Messenger)
* **Role:** Static morning mail courier & rare seed forager.
* **Behavior:** Perches loyally on the mailbox post at dawn. Does not follow Rowan into the wilderness. Delivers letters, town bulletin notices, and mysterious wild seeds gathered from the Whisperwood glades.

### 🐱 Mallow (Calico Hearth Familiar)
* **Role:** Eatery guardian & cozy companion.
* **Behavior:** Sought shelter beside the tavern hearth during a stormy gale and claimed it as home. Naps peacefully on the wooden counter and rubs affectionately against dining patrons.

### 👵 Grandmother Amalia (Guiding Legacy & Spirit)
* **Role:** Late culinary artisan whose handwritten grimoire guides Rowan.
* **Core Philosophy:** *"Cooking is not about showing off technical virtuosity; it is about making the soul who eats your meal feel that they are worthy of living and being loved."*

## 2. Oakhaven Citizens & Narrative Depth

---

### ⚒️ Boris Barnaby (Master Blacksmith) & ⛏️ Bram Barnaby (Deep Mine Forager)
> **Real-world Theme:** Dysfunctional brotherly bond — wounded male pride, inability to communicate love, and lingering trauma.

* **Boris Barnaby (Elder Brother - Age 52):**
  - *Psychological Wound:* A mine collapse on Floor 40 ten years ago crushed Bram's leg. Boris blamed himself for calling the blast, abandoned cave expeditions, and turned into an overbearing, controlling guardian who treats Bram like a fragile cripple.
  - *Signature Comfort Dish:* **Dwarf Ironforge Goulash (Slow-braised Beef & Beet Stew)** — the hearty recipe their late mother cooked for them in their youth.
* **Bram Barnaby (Younger Brother - Age 45):**
  - *Psychological Wound:* Resents being pitied. Bram dives recklessly into deeper mine shafts not out of greed, but to scream in Boris's face that he is still an independent, capable man who refuses pity.
  - *Signature Comfort Dish:* **Miner's Sourdough Loaf & Earth Root Stew**.
* **Heart Events Arc:**
  - *2 Hearts:* Heated argument at the anvil; Boris confiscates Bram's custom pickaxe, prompting Bram to storm off to the riverside in bitter silence.
  - *4 Hearts:* Rowan discovers Bram massaging his throbbing leg in the cold shadows of Floor 20, confessing that hearing Boris frantically hammer away rocks for 3 days was the only thing that kept him breathing under the rubble.
  - *6 Hearts:* Penny tasks Rowan with uncovering an old tin box containing their mother's original goulash recipe and a letter pleading for them to support one another.
  - *8 Hearts (Reconciliation Feast):* Rowan hosts a family dinner serving piping-hot Goulash. The hardened dwarven brothers weep openly, shattering a decade of estrangement and unlocking the **Advanced Refining Forge**.

---

### 🌾 Grandma Maeve (Elder Herbalist) & 🕯️ Little Kael (Matchstick Boy)
> **Real-world Theme:** Found family beyond bloodlines; the fragility of elder years and an orphan's terror of abandonment.

* **Grandma Maeve (Age 74):**
  - *Psychological Wound:* Lost her entire family to miasma plague 30 years ago. Crippling osteoarthritis torments her every winter, but she secretly refuses expensive medicines, saving every copper coin for Kael's future education and apprenticeship.
  - *Signature Comfort Dish:* **Sweet Pumpkin Lotus Potage & Sun Daisy Tea**.
* **Little Kael (Age 10):**
  - *Psychological Wound:* Shivered on frozen riverbanks peddling matches before Maeve took him in. Haunted by acute abandonment trauma: terrified that if Maeve passes away, he will be cast back into the freezing dark. Overworks himself relentlessly to prove he is worth keeping.
  - *Signature Comfort Dish:* **Seven-Color Faerie Birthday Cake** (Kael never knew his biological birthday; Maeve celebrates the day she adopted him).
* **Heart Events Arc:**
  - *3 Hearts:* Kael breaks down crying after dropping seed packets into muddy gutters; Rowan helps him wash the seeds and serves warm comforting broth.
  - *6 Hearts (Winter Blizzard):* Maeve suffers sudden chest pain during a gale. Kael braves the storm to pound on Rowan's tavern door, leading to a desperate midnight medical rescue.
  - *8 Hearts (The Hidden Passbook):* Kael uncovers Maeve's secret savings labeled *"For Kael's Carpentry Apprenticeship"*. The two embrace in tears. Unlocks the **Four-Seasons Seed Nursery**.

---

### 🛡️ Adventurer's Guild Leadership: Thorne & Valerie
> **Real-world Theme:** Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD), survivor's guilt, and finding a renewed sense of purpose.

* **Guildmaster Thorne (Age 50):**
  - *Psychological Wound:* Veteran vanguard captain bearing an iron prosthetic leg. Fifteen years ago, a catastrophic command error in the Sunken Necropolis wiped out his entire 18-soldier scouting squad. He founded the Oakhaven Rescue Outpost with a binding oath: *"No one is ever left behind in the dark."*
  - *Signature Comfort Dish:* **Campfire Spiced Jerky Stew**.
* **Dispatcher Valerie (Age 32 - Lost & Found Counter):**
  - *Psychological Wound:* Retired sharpshooter who suffered permanent unilateral hearing loss and severe acoustic shock (PTSD) from high-explosive ordnance. Meticulously catalogs lost adventurer belongings behind a quiet desk, secretly hoping to recover relics of fallen comrades.
  - *Signature Comfort Dish:* **Snow Fungus & Pork Rib Broth**.
* **Heart Events Arc:**
  - *4 Hearts:* Thorne stands silently in the rain before the Ancient Memorial Altar at the forest edge on the anniversary of his lost battalion.
  - *8 Hearts (Healing the Survivors):* Rowan cooks a nutritious frontline feast. Thorne and Valerie finally release the ghosts of their past, reducing rescue penalties by 50% and unlocking the **Deep-Mine Rescue Strike Force**.

---

### 🔭 Professor Orion & 📚 Clara (Astronomy Tower)
> **Real-world Theme:** Cognitive decline / early-stage Alzheimer's, caregiver burnout, and filial sacrifice.

* **Professor Orion (Age 68):**
  - *Psychological Wound:* Slowly losing his memory to degenerative dementia. While forgetting village names and basic daily routines, he stays awake night after night beside his brass telescope, desperately searching for the fleeting comet he named after his late wife, Elise, before his mind empties completely.
  - *Signature Comfort Dish:* **Astral Berry Glazed Tart**.
* **Clara (Age 24 - Head Librarian):**
  - *Psychological Wound:* Tore up an elite university scholarship in Solaria to stay behind and care for her fading father. Constantly oscillates between profound filial love and suffocating grief over her forfeited youth.
  - *Signature Comfort Dish:* **Earl Grey Jasmine Tea & Crispy Butter Biscuits**.
* **Heart Events Arc:**
  - *4 Hearts:* Orion wanders into the moonlit fog, mistaking distant lanterns for his deceased wife; Clara tracks him down in tears.
  - *8 Hearts (Constellation of Remembrance):* Rowan and Clara reconstruct Elise's star charts; Orion experiences a moment of lucid clarity, holding Clara's hand in heartfelt gratitude. Unlocks the **Ancient Mythic Recipe Archives**.

---

### ⚓ Captain Garrick & 🎣 Young Finn
* **Garrick (Age 70):** Color-blind, weather-beaten sky-sailor who feels like an obsolete piece of driftwood stranded ashore.
* **Finn (Age 12):** Determined grandson fishing day and night to earn back the heirloom Silver Compass Garrick pawned to pay for his late father's medicine.
* **Signature Comfort Dish:** **Smoked Forest Salmon & Crisp Frostmint Tea**.

---

### 🌿 Lumi (Whisperwood Glade Kemomimi-Elf)
* **Psychological Wound:** Unique hybrid bearing both snow-fox ears and elven ears. Ostracized by both woodland fae and humans as an anomaly. Craves human warmth yet terrified of rejection.
* **Signature Comfort Dish:** **Calming Lotus Infusion & Steamed Coconut Rice Cakes**.

---

### 🧺 Penny (Itinerant Pastry Courier)
* **Psychological Wound:** Orphaned niece raised awkwardly by bachelor uncles Boris and Bram. Yearns to build an independent motorized bakery cart.
* **Signature Comfort Dish:** **Wild Berry Shortcake & Chilled Strawberry Infusion**.

---

### 🩺 Aurelia (Town Medic) & ⚔️ Marlo (Nightwatch Sentinel)
* **Aurelia:** Disillusioned physician who fled royal medical corruption to treat the impoverished, struggling with scarce supplies.
* **Marlo:** Self-effacing nightwatchman who sends 90% of his pay to support younger siblings back home, finding his only solace at Rowan's midnight tavern table.

## 3. Wandering Patrons & Distant Visitors

* **Elora & Sylvan:** Tremor-afflicted royal dressmaker and devoted botanist seeking ancient remedies to preserve her artisan hands.
* **Valen:** Pacifist, vegetarian vampire aristocrat seeking soothing, non-garlic floral infusions.
* **Kassandra:** Exiled meteor witch traversing atmospheric currents on a broom running low on stardust mana.
* **Chrono:** Ancient clockwork automaton seeking chamomile lubrication to soften millennia-old gears.
* **Lapis:** Deep-sea mermaid fascinated by terrestrial warm baked breads.
