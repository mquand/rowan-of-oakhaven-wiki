# 👥 Character Roster — Inhabitants of Oakhaven & The Five Realms

> **Game:** Rowan of Oakhaven  
> Meet the central characters, heartwarming companions, and valley patrons you will converse with and serve cozy teas and pastries to every twilight.

---

## 1. Protagonist & Cherished Companions

### 🌸 Rowan (Keeper of Oakhaven Cafe & Bakery)
* **Role:** Player-controlled protagonist.
* **Background:** A gifted young witch who recently graduated from the Royal Arcane Academy, burned out from detached academic pressure. She returns to Oakhaven to reopen her late grandmother Amalia's tea salon, guided by deep empathy and skillful artisan hands.
* **Appearance:** Pinned dried blossoms in her hair, linen flour-dusted apron, and warm, attentive eyes.

### 🦉 Pip (Pygmy Owl Messenger)
* **Role:** Night mail courier & rare botanical seed forager.
* **Background:** An intelligent, plump little owl who scours the nocturnal Whisperwood canopies, bringing mysterious forgotten seeds to Rowan's mailbox at dawn.

### 🐱 Mallow (Calico Hearth Familiar)
* **Role:** Shop guardian & cuddly good-luck mascot.
* **Background:** A chubby calico cat who sought shelter in the tea parlor during a stormy gale and decided to stay forever. Loves dozing beside warm oil lanterns and receiving gentle chin scratches.

### 👵 Grandmother Amalia (Legacy / Guiding Spirit)
* **Role:** Legendary tea artisan who bequeathed the shop and her secret grimoire.
* **Background:** Amalia's tender maternal presence lives on through handwritten grimoire margins, experimental sketches, and gentle memory echoes.

## 2. Inhabitants of Oakhaven Town

### 🌿 Lumi (Whisperwood Glade Spirit)
* **Identity:** Unique Kemomimi-Elf spirit — fluffy white snow fox ears atop jade twin-tails, long pointed fae ears, and a soft red knitted scarf.
* **Personality:** Shy, easily overwhelmed by crowds, secretly yearning for gentle companionship.
* **Favorite Brew:** A refreshing cup of *Calming Lotus Tea* to quiet her fluttering thoughts.

### ⚒️ Boris Barnaby (Master Blacksmith)
* **Identity:** Sturdy dwarven smith renowned for his twin-braided russet beard and soot-dusted apron.
* **Personality:** Gruff, frank, and hardened by decades at the anvil, yet hiding a solitary heart.
* **Favorite Morsel:** Piping-hot *Forest Spirit Cinnamon Rolls* and warming spiced ginger tea.

### 🌾 Maeve (Elder Herb Cultivator)
* **Identity:** Kindly town herbalist and foster grandmother to young Kael.
* **Personality:** Cheerful and endlessly generous, though often overworking her aching back in the garden.
* **Favorite Treat:** *Bioluminescent Berry Scones* and revitalizing Sun Daisy tea.

### 🕯️ Kael (Apprentice Match Vendor)
* **Identity:** Orphaned boy wandering the riverbank market, lovingly taken in by Maeve.
* **Personality:** Polite, observant, and deeply worried about Grandma Maeve's failing health.
* **Favorite Gift:** *Faerie Birthday Cake* baked with tender warmth.

### ⛏️ Bram (Crystal Mine Forager)
* **Identity:** Veteran deep-shaft miner; younger brother of blacksmith Boris.
* **Personality:** Craggy and stubborn, loves bantering with his brother but fiercely loyal to family.
* **Favorite Brew:** Earthy root decoctions and rustic barley biscuits.

### 🧺 Penny (Wandering Pastry Hawker)
* **Identity:** Nimble young pastry vendor; niece to Boris and Bram.
* **Personality:** Energetic, cheerful, and brimming with eccentric baking brainstorms.
* **Favorite Drink:** Chilled wild berry iced tea and fluffy cream puffs.

### 🔭 Professor Orion (Sleepless Astrologer)
* **Identity:** Hilltop observatory astronomer; Clara's father.
* **Personality:** Absentminded scholar captivated by constellations, frequently forgetting meals and sleep.
* **Favorite Brew:** Honey chamomile tranquil tea that allows his overactive mind to rest.

### 📚 Clara (Town Archivist & Librarian)
* **Identity:** Curator of the Oakhaven archives; Orion's daughter.
* **Personality:** Organized, poised, constantly trekking up the observatory hill to make sure her father eats.
* **Favorite Cup:** Fragrant Earl Grey and crisp shortbread butter cookies.

### ⚓ Captain Garrick (Veteran Cloud-Sea Mariner)
* **Identity:** Weathered sea captain who retired his oars; grandfather to Finn.
* **Personality:** Storyteller who loves regaling tavern patrons with tales of uncharted floating isles.
* **Favorite Cup:** Spiced wild cinnamon tea tinged with ocean memories.

### 🎣 Finn (Star-Angler Boy)
* **Identity:** Spirited boy fishing the mountain lakes; Garrick's grandson.
* **Personality:** Inquisitive and passionate about catching glowing aquatic creatures.
* **Favorite Refreshment:** Glacial peppermint iced tea.

## 3. Nomads & Outlying Realm Patrons

* **Elora:** Gala robe seamstress tailoring moonlight velvet robes in Sylva-Fae.
* **Sylvan:** Monocled master botanist researching ancient canopy roots.
* **Marlo:** Stalwart town watchman patrolling through freezing snowy night shifts.
* **Aurelia:** Caring clinic apothecary nurturing town stamina and wellness.
* **Valen:** Aristocratic vegetarian vampire who avoids garlic but adores sweet ruby berry tea.
* **Kassandra:** Exiled comet sorceress drifting through cloud horizons.
* **Chrono:** Ancient clockwork automaton requiring floral oil lubricant for his gears.
* **Lapis:** Floating air-bubble mermaid fascinated by warm, crispy terrestrial pastries.
