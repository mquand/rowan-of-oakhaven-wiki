> **Game:** Rowan of Oakhaven

## World Map & Crossroads

The world of Rowan of Oakhaven unfolds across **Five Great Realms**, each possessing its own architecture, ecosystems, and unique cultures. Oakhaven — Rowan's tranquil starting hamlet — sits right at the heart of this world, serving as the crossroads connecting every journey.

## The Five Realms

### 🌿 Realm 1 — Oakhaven (Central Valley)
**Overview:** The tranquil village where Rowan reopens her grandmother Amalia's tea salon. The Whisperwood borders the north, and the crystal mines descend to the east.  
**Key Locations:** Starlight Tea Shop, Boris's Forge, Sunday Nautilus Pier, Professor Orion's Observatory, Finn's Shallows  
**Inhabitants:** Townsfolk, forest glade spirits, dwarven blacksmiths, familiar creatures  

### ⚒️ Realm 2 — Khaz-Drakor (Dwarven Megalopolis)
**Overview:** Subterranean dwarven kingdom carved thousands of meters into granite bedrock, boasting deep mineral veins and roaring geothermal forges.  
**Features:** Hand-carved masonry, gold-lined tram tunnels, bustling ore bazaars, master smith guilds  
**Inhabitants:** Dwarven miners, runic metalworkers, mechanical alchemists  

### 🌲 Realm 3 — Sylva-Fae (The Ancient Canopy)
**Overview:** Mythical canopy forest where elven folk dwell harmoniously among thousand-year-old boughs. Rare luminescent botanicals thrive here alone.  
**Features:** Treehouse acorn dwellings, bioluminescent spider-silk walkways, herb conservatories, fairy fountains  
**Inhabitants:** Elven rangers, master botanists, fresh stream merfolk  

### 🏯 Realm 4 — Solaria (The Capital of Light)
**Overview:** The magnificent white-marble metropolis of the sorcery empire where Rowan once studied at the Arcane Academy. High politics and boundless commerce.  
**Features:** Spire academies, wizard bazaars, underground archives, Grand Tea Guild arenas  
**Inhabitants:** Aristocratic spellcasters, royal scholars, international merchants  

### ✨ Realm 5 — Aethelgard (The Floating Elysium)
**Overview:** Legendary floating islands drifting above clouds where the boundary between mortals and cosmic starlight gently blurs. Unlocked near the story's climax.  
**Features:** Floating stardust crystals, celestial meadows, shrines of antiquity  
**Inhabitants:** Ancestral spirits, cloud guardians, radiant astral fauna
